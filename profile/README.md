### Your app works. Now it has to survive real customers.

AI app builders are genuinely good at the first draft. They are systematically weaker on the
last 30%:

- **Authorization correctness** — can the right people reach the right things, and nobody else?
- **Data isolation** — is one customer's data actually separated from another's?
- **Secrets handling** — are API keys, tokens and credentials kept out of the client bundle?
- **Backup and restore** — has anyone verified the restore, not just the backup?
- **Dependency review**, **tests**, **CI/CD** and **monitoring**

That gap is not a criticism of the tools. It is simply where they stop and where we start.
